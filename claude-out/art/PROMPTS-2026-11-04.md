# Kingdom Nights — art prompt pack (2026-11-04)

For Higgsfield (Omar runs these in the app; the connector is read-only from a cloud session) or any image model.
Canva first drafts of the first three are referenced at the bottom. Roblox sizes: **game icon 512×512** (also shown at
~64 px in lists, so one big readable shape), **thumbnails 1920×1080** (up to 10; test 3), **pass / product icons
512×512** (shown ~150 px in the store). Everything is original art; nothing may reference Thronefall by name or look.

**House style line to paste in front of every prompt:**
> stylized low-poly painterly game art, clean silhouettes, warm torchlight against a cold dusk-blue sky, dark stone and
> dark-red banners with gold trim, high contrast, no text, no letters, no watermark

## 1. Game icon (make 3 variants, A/B test during soft launch)
- **A — the keep:** a lone stone castle keep on a hill under a rising blood-red moon, torch-lit battlements, one tiny knight silhouette on the wall. Centre composition, keep fills 60% of the frame.
- **B — the horde:** low angle from the road: a wall of goblins and an ogre charging toward the camera, a castle tower firing glowing arrows above them. Motion, danger, one strong diagonal.
- **C — the hero:** a knight on a warhorse rearing on a wall at night, torches below, blood moon behind. Readable silhouette, gold on dark.
Icon rules: no small details, one light source, test it at 64 px before uploading.

## 2. Thumbnails (16:9)
1. **Night siege:** four torch-lit roads converging on a hilltop fort, hordes on every road, towers firing bolts, a knight on a warhorse in the foreground. (Canva draft below.)
2. **Co-op moment:** two champions back to back on a wall, one holding a downed teammate up (the revive), monsters climbing the wall below. Sells co-op.
3. **The collection:** six heroes lined up on castle steps (stone tank, bow captain, hound handler, torchbearer, sky lancer with a winged mount, a golem behind), each a different colour, gold banners. Sells the roster.
4. **Endless:** a ruined watchtower at dusk, an impossibly large horde filling the whole valley below, one brazier still lit. "NIGHT 40" energy, no text.
